# ABTalksOnAI Podcast

## Register
brand

## Platform
web

## Users
Curious students, working professionals, builders and founders exploring AI and its impact on their work.

## Purpose
A separate podcast website, confirmed by the user, for discovering and watching ABTalksOnAI conversations hosted by Anil Bajpai. This website is a podcast destination within the wider ABTalksOnAI brand.

## Positioning
Understand AI through conversations with the people building, investing in and working with it.

## Conversion & proof
Primary action: watch an episode. Secondary actions: browse episodes, listen on Apple Podcasts, or enquire about being a guest. Episode links, names and durations were checked on the official YouTube channel on 12 October 2026. No audience statistics or testimonials are used.

## Brand Personality
Curious, candid, energetic. A studio after sunset, with a lit recording console and human faces. Navy backgrounds, orange accents and clear typography carry this scene.

## References
Trainplex: dark canvas, orange emphasis, floating navigation and a dimensional hero. Kevin Surace: confident narrative, video and clear actions. StringTune: word reveals, photo drift and animated strike-through. Vengeance UI and 21st.dev: component inspiration. Koboyo: a small cursor-responsive page mascot. Emil design engineering: responsive motion, custom easing, reduced-motion behavior.

## Design Principles
Episodes are the product. Human conversations lead the imagery. Motion must serve exploration. Real names and real links take priority over decorative proof. Every primary action must work on phones and keyboards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, readable contrast, native dialogs, visible focus, responsive layout and reduced-motion support. Canvas art is decorative and never required to read or navigate.

## Revision, 13 October 2026
The user rejected the first visual design and supplied Anil Bajpai's LinkedIn profile. The revision uses his real 800-pixel profile portrait, downloaded from the profile photo viewer, and his self-reported enterprise experience and agentic AI focus. The photograph retains its identity; grayscale is a CSS presentation treatment. Employer endorsements and audience counts are not implied. Source: https://www.linkedin.com/in/anil-bajpai/ (read in Chrome on 13 October 2026).

## Research-approved rebuild
The user approved applying the podcast-site research and requested a changed website. React migration enables integrating real source components from Arc UI, Skecher UI, Space UI and Componentry.
